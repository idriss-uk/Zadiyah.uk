Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide operational reporting.

2. How We Use Cookies

Zadiyah (zadiyah.uk) uses cookies for:

  • Essential Functions: Maintaining shopping cart items and checkout navigation.
  • Performance & Analytics: Understanding how visitors interact with our storefront to improve performance.
  • Preferences: Remembering your basic site interactions across sessions.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services integrated on our site, such as payment gateways (Stripe) and basic analytics, may place cookies on your browser to facilitate secure payments and track anonymous store metrics.

4. Managing Cookies

You can choose to disable or block cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact store checkout functionality.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about our Cookie Policy, reach out to us at info@zadiyah.uk.
