The ZADIYAH Story

Born from
British Rain

London Rain was conceived during an October evening walk across Waterloo Bridge — that rare moment when the city pauses beneath a violet sky and the first heavy drops of rain transform the streets into rivers of reflected gold.

Every bottle is a small-batch formulation, hand-poured in our East London atelier. We source only the finest cruelty-free, ethically traded ingredients — from Calabrian bergamot to sustainably harvested Haitian vetiver — ensuring each spray tells an honest story.

We believe in the power of scent to transcend the ordinary. London Rain isn't merely a fragrance. It's a sensory memoir — an invitation to carry the city's most poetic moment with you, wherever you go.
