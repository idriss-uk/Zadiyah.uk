Olfactory Architecture

The Scent Pyramid

"The smell of rain, bottled."

A meticulously composed trilogy of olfactory layers, unfolding like rain upon warm stone.

Top Notes • 0–30 Mins

Petrichor & Bergamot

Bitter orange, crisp morning ozone, and the fresh mineral aroma of raindrops hitting dry London pavement.

Heart Notes • 30 Mins – 4 Hours

Clary Sage & Geranium

Aromatic English herbs, herbal greens, and fresh floral undertones that evoke rain-swept gardens.

Base Notes • 4–8+ Hours

Cedarwood & Oakmoss

Warm amber, Haitian vetiver, and rain-soaked earth providing a long-lasting, sophisticated foundation.

The ZADIYAH Story

Born from
British Rain

London Rain was conceived during an October evening walk across Waterloo Bridge — that rare moment when the city pauses beneath a violet sky and the first heavy drops of rain transform the streets into rivers of reflected gold.

Every bottle is a small-batch formulation hand-poured in the United Kingdom. We source ethically traded ingredients — from Calabrian bergamot to sustainably harvested Haitian vetiver — ensuring each spray tells an honest story.

Small-Batch Cruelty-Free Hand-Poured in the UK

Application

The Daily Ritual

01

Pulse Points

Spray 2–3 inches away onto pulse points: collarbones, inner wrists, and behind the ears.

02

Allow to Settle

Avoid rubbing wrists together. Let the top notes of ozonic bergamot naturally evolve on your skin.

03

Lingering Trail

The cedarwood and vetiver base will develop into an intimate, warm trail lasting 6 to 8 hours.
